## Royalty Structure

### Running Royalties
**Royalty Rates** (Two-model structure):
- **11% of [wholesale price]**; OR
- **6% of [retail price]**

This is a **two-model royalty structure** (wholesale and retail), similar to Charmy Chan but simpler than the three-model approach.

**Net Sales Definition**: 
- Gross sales of the Licensed Products
- Less only actual and verifiable chargebacks, refunds, and returns for damaged or defective Licensed Products
- Cumulative deductions shall not exceed 10% of accumulated gross sales earned during the relevant quarter
- No deductions for costs incurred in manufacturing, inspecting, packing, advertising, or distribution
- No deductions for taxes, duties, insurance, or freight
- No deductions for uncollectable accounts
- No deductions for currency exchange

### Reporting and Payment Schedule
- **Reporting Deadline**: On or before the 30th day of April, July, October, and January
- **Report Contents**: Complete and accurate statement showing all sales details, units sold, gross prices, and royalties due

### Payment Terms
- **Payment Timing**: Due and payable on the last day of the calendar month immediately following each License Quarter
- **Payment Method**: To Licensing Agent's bank account
- **Late Payment Interest**: 10% per annum from the date payment becomes due until date of payment in full

## Minimum Guarantee

### Amount and Schedule
- **Minimum Guarantee**: USD 12,000
- **Payment Schedule**: Upon signing the contract
- **Licensed Term**: 1st December, 2024 to 31st March, 2026

### MG Recoupment
- Minimum Guarantee is recoupable against any Running Royalties payable by the Licensee under this Agreement
- Once the Minimum Guarantee is fully recouped, the Licensee shall pay the appropriate Running Royalties

### MG Forfeiture Conditions
The Licensee agrees that the Minimum Guarantee shall be forfeited by the Licensing Agent in the following events:
- (i) Total amount of Running Royalties received is less than the Minimum Guarantee
- (ii) Licensee fails to commence production of the Licensed Products on or before 1st April, 2025
- (iii) Licensee fails to commence public distribution of the Licensed Products on or before 1st June, 2025

## Central Marketing Fund (CMF)

**Important**: The CMF is NOT applicable in this license agreement. The Licensee shall not contribute this CMF payment to Sekiguchi and the Licensing Agent.

## Implementation Notes

The platform must support flexible contract structures that accommodate:
- Different numbers of royalty rate models (1, 2, or 3)
- Different MG amounts and payment schedules
- Different contract durations and commencement dates
- Optional CMF contributions
- Both exclusive and non-exclusive territories
- Varying reporting deadlines (25-30 days)
- Different late payment interest rates (5-10%)
