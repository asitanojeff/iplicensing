# Charmy Chan Style Guide Analysis

## Brand Overview
- **IP Name**: Charmy Chan (なかよしチャーミー)
- **Licensor**: Kodama Toy Co., Ltd. (児玉産業TOY株式会社)
- **Licensed by**: EL Brand
- **Character**: Charmy Chan - A cute character with bear ears
- **Founded**: 1930 (Kodama Celluloid Co.)
- **Current**: Kodama Sangyo Toy Co., Ltd.

## Character System
The character has two main variants:

### 1. Pom-pom Variant (Pink/Brown)
- **Main Color**: Pink (PANTONE C)
- **Hair**: Brown/Reddish
- **Ribbon Tie**: Blue
- **Eyes**: Fewer and shorter eyebrows, 3 white dots in the eye
- **Ears**: Pom-pom style (fluffy balls on top)

### 2. Bear Ear Variant (Yellow/Pink)
- **Main Color**: Yellow (PANTONE C)
- **Hair**: Pink/Magenta
- **Ribbon Tie**: Coral/Orange
- **Eyes**: More and longer eyebrows, 2 white dots in the eye
- **Ears**: Bear ears (larger, more defined)

## Color Palette (Pantone C)
### Main Colors
- **Pink**: PANTONE 16-1546 (Main Body - Pom-pom variant)
- **Pink Magenta**: PANTONE 17-1564 (Hair - Bear variant)
- **Yellow**: PANTONE 12-0605 (Main Body - Bear variant)
- **Yellow Gold**: PANTONE 13-0755 (Accents)
- **Light Yellow**: PANTONE 12-0605 (Lighter accents)

### Accent Colors
- **Blue**: PANTONE 17-4535 (Ribbon ties, accents)
- **Dark Blue**: PANTONE 19-3952 (Darker accents)
- **Brown**: PANTONE 16-1520 (Hair - Pom-pom variant)
- **Dark Brown**: PANTONE 18-1546 (Hair details)
- **Coral/Orange**: PANTONE 16-1546 (Ribbon - Bear variant)
- **Light Peach**: PANTONE 13-1520 (Face)
- **Darker Peach**: PANTONE 16-1520 (Face shading)
- **Light Blue**: PANTONE 12-0605 (Lace cap)

## Design Elements
- **Lace Cape/Collar**: White lace trim around neck
- **Knit Patterns**: 
  - Knit Pattern-01: Pink knit with geometric design
  - Knit Pattern-02: Yellow knit with geometric design
  - Additional patterns for customization
- **Body Proportions**: Cute, round proportions with oversized head
- **Pose Variations**: Multiple poses available (standing, jumping, sitting, etc.)

## Asset Categories for IP Bank
1. **Character Models** (Front, Back, Side views)
2. **Pose Variations** (Different expressions and positions)
3. **Color Variants** (Pink/Pom-pom and Yellow/Bear)
4. **Knit Patterns** (Customizable patterns for clothing)
5. **Accessories** (Lace caps, ribbons, etc.)
6. **Background Elements** (Clouds, grass, etc.)

## Brand Guidelines Key Points
- Maintain consistent color palette (use Pantone C colors)
- Character proportions must remain consistent
- Both variants must be recognizable as Charmy Chan
- Lace collar/cape is a signature element
- Character is suitable for toys, merchandise, and licensing
- Japanese heritage (Tokyo/Japan emphasis)
