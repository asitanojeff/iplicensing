## Licensed Territory
**Thailand** (Exclusive)

## Contract Duration

### Licensed Term
- **Start Date**: 15th July, 2025
- **End Date**: 31st March, 2026
- **Duration**: Approximately 8.5 months
- **License Commencement Date**: Any date within the Licensed Term as agreed by the Parties
- **Market Commencement Date**: 1st November, 2025
- **Production Commencement Date**: 1st October, 2025

## Royalty Structure

### Running Royalties
**Royalty Rate**: 7% of Net Sales, based on the retail price of the Licensed Products

**Net Sales Definition**: 
- Gross sales of the Licensed Products
- Less only actual and verifiable chargebacks, refunds, and returns for damaged or defective Licensed Products
- No deductions for costs incurred in manufacturing, inspecting, packing, advertising, or distribution
- No deductions for taxes, duties, insurance, or freight
- No deductions for uncollectable accounts
- No deductions for currency exchange

### Reporting and Payment Schedule
- **Reporting Deadline**: On or before the 30th day of April, July, October, and January
- **Report Contents**: Complete and accurate statement showing:
  - Description of items
  - Number and/or style of items
  - Number of units sold
  - Gross invoice price per item
  - Total gross sales price
  - Total Running Royalties paid by Licensee to Licensing Agent
  - CMF (Central Marketing Fund) if applicable

### Payment Terms
- **Payment Timing**: Due and payable on the last day of the calendar month immediately following the First License Quarter or such License Quarter
- **Payment Method**: To Licensing Agent's bank account (details provided)
- **Late Payment Interest**: 10% per annum from the date payment becomes due until date of payment in full by the Licensee

- **Bank Name**: DBS Bank (Hong Kong) Limited
- **Bank Address**: 11/F, The Center, 99 Queen's Road Central, Central, Hong Kong
- **Account Number**: 001433824

### Tax Responsibility
- Licensee pays all taxes including inter alia, value added taxes, withholding taxes, consumers' taxes
- Any fees and charges, bank charges levied on any amounts due and payable under this Agreement
- Without limitation: Minimum Guarantee, Running Royalty, and all taxes, bank commissions and handling charges
- All resulting penalties and interest imposed on the Licensing Agent shall be borne by the Licensee

### Royalty Guarantee
- Licensee guarantees that the total amount of Running Royalties to be paid during the Licensed Term shall not be less than the Minimum Guarantee
- If total Running Royalties received before expiration or termination is less than the Minimum Guarantee, the Licensee shall forfeit the difference
- Licensee agrees that the Minimum Guarantee shall be forfeited by the Licensing Agent in the following events:
  - (i) Total amount of Running Royalties received is less than the Minimum Guarantee
  - (ii) Licensee fails to commence production of the Licensed Products on or before the Production Commencement Date
  - (iii) Licensee fails to commence public distribution of the Licensed Products on or before the Marketing Commencement Date

### No Obligation to Return
- The Licensing Agent and Sekiguchi are not obliged to return paid Royalties to the Licensee for any reason whatsoever

## Minimum Guarantee

### Amount and Schedule
- **Minimum Guarantee**: USD 5,000
- **Payment Schedule**: Upon sign the contract
- **Licensed Term**: 15th July, 2025 to 31st March, 2026

### MG Recoupment
- Minimum Guarantee is recoupable against any Running Royalties payable by the Licensee under this Agreement
- Once the Minimum Guarantee is fully recouped, the Licensee shall pay to the Licensing Agent the appropriate Running Royalties in accordance with Clause 7 hereof

## Central Marketing Fund (CMF)

### CMF Administration
- A Central Marketing Fund ("CMF") shall be administered by Sekiguchi and the Licensing Agent
- CMF shall be expended for consumer promotion, advertising and marketing of the Licensed Products
- Amounts and manner decided by Sekiguchi and the Licensing Agent (Licensee has no say)

### CMF Contributions
- The Licensee shall make payments into the CMF (the "CMF Contributions")
- The Licensing Agent is responsible for collecting CMF Contributions from the Licensee
- Licensee's CMF contribution is agreed separately (not specified in this excerpt)

## Security Labels

### Label Requirements
- Licensee undertakes the obligation to order security labels at its own cost including courier fee from the Licensing Agent directly
- All Licensed Products or its packaging must properly stick the security labels which is officially approved and licensed by Licensor

### Label Compliance
- First Occurrence: If Licensor or Licensing Agent identifies missing or improperly affixed security label(s) on licensed products or their packaging and the Licensee is unable to provide reasonable explanation, the Licensee shall incur a penalty of USD 10,000 per occurrence
- Second Occurrence: If a second violation occurs, the Licensee may receive a formal warning letter. The Licensor may receive a formal warning letter. The Licensor may terminate the agreement in the event of recurring non-compliance, and further penalties may apply as deemed necessary

## Approval and Brand Control

### Pre-Production Samples
- Licensee shall provide pre-production samples for approval at own cost
- Samples: Six (6) for each item in each color, version or other variant

### Design Materials and Approval
- **Design Materials**: 
  - Style guide provided and/or approved by Sekiguchi from time to time
  - Sample of which is exhibited in Schedule B
  - Character list attached in Schedule D

- **Associated Materials**: All materials associated with or supplied to consumers with the Licensed Products, including but not limited to labels, wrappings, packages, containers, display, publicity, advertising and marketing materials, and any other materials and/or services

### Approval Process
- All submissions shall be complete and in format reasonably required by the Licensor
- Licensee shall submit for prior written approval:
  - Product designs and concepts
  - Prototypes and samples
  - Packaging and labeling
  - Advertising and marketing materials

- **Response Time**: Licensor shall use reasonable efforts to respond within ten (10) working days
- **Revision Period**: Upon revision request, review period recommences upon resubmission
- **Approval Requirement**: No approval shall be deemed granted unless expressly given in writing
- **Production Restriction**: Licensee shall not proceed to production, distribution, or sale without approval
- **Final Authority**: IP Owner retains final approval rights
